## 🎯 Quick Overview

**Gpowerpay Platform** consists of two main applications:

### 👥 Customer App (USER_PLAN.md)
- 🔐 Social & Email/Password Authentication
- 💰 Digital Wallet with Paystack Integration
- 🛍️ Dual Market System (Kilo & Carton)
- 🛒 Shopping Cart with Smart Payment
- 🚚 Home Delivery or Store Pickup
- 📦 Order Tracking & Management
- 👤 User-Friendly Interface (Ages 25+)

### 👨‍💼 Admin System (ADMIN_PLAN.md)
- 🔐 Role-Based Access (Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Rider)
- 📦 Order Management & Assignment
- 🏍️ Rider Management & Tracking
- 👥 Customer Management
- 📊 Reports & Analytics
- 🗺️ Delivery Zone Configuration
- ⚙️ System Settings

## 🎯 User Roles

### Customer Roles
- **Customer**: Regular shoppers with wallet and order access

### Admin Roles
- **Super Admin**: Full system access, manage Support users, financial control
- **Support**: Manage orders, riders, products, customers (limited permissions)
- **Rider**: Mobile/web access to assigned deliveries
